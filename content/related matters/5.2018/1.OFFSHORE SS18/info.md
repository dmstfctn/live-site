OFFSHORE SS18<span class="dc-hide-on-large"><br>2018</span>
Installation, dimensions variable
Beach towel 83x143cm, double sided silkscreen
Swimsuit, Slinky Matt Lycra
Trunks, Lycra Soft Sheen
E-shop

Exhibited: HKW (Berlin), Luma (Arles), Arter (Istanbul), C-MINE (Genk), Z33 (Hasselt)<span class="dc-hide-on-large"><br><br></span>

OFFSHORE SS18 was a limited edition beachwear collection sold through an offshore corporate structure set up by the artists. Each purchase through an <a href="http://shop.dmstfctn.net/" target="_blank">online shop</a> activated a functional tax avoidance scheme, moving revenue through a set of offshre companies to avoid taxation. Details of the movement are printed on the apparel as diagrams. The tax saved were distributed to members of the public who had invested in the structure during a <a href="https://youtu.be/4ef4IypFNns" target="_blank">performance</a> at transmediale festival 2018, in Berlin.<br>
